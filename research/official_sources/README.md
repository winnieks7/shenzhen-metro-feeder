# Official documentary sources

The sources establish historical route names, stops and stated service purposes. They do not independently validate walking access, travel time, frequency, passenger demand or the dataset's geometry.

| Source | Period / publication | Use | URL |
|---|---|---|---|
| Shenzhen bus-route and stop registry | April 2025; published May 20, 2025 | Extracted factual table `shenzhen_bus_april2025.csv`; label comparison and 22-station documentary check | https://www.sz.gov.cn/xxgk/sydwxxgk/jt/gj/ywxx/gjlx/content/post_12183737.html |
| Shenzhen bus-route and stop registry | May 2025; published June 9, 2025 | Named destination stops for the three cases | https://jtys.sz.gov.cn/ydmh/jtcx/gjcx_180959/gjfw/content/post_12216650.html |
| Targeted bus services and last-mile travel | June 10, 2025; Shenzhen Special Zone Daily, reprinted by the transport bureau | Documentary service context for hospitals and employment destinations | https://jtys.sz.gov.cn/gkmlpt/content/12/12221/post_12221267.html |
| Metro opening notice | December 2024 | Historical metro scope | https://www.sz.gov.cn/gkmlpt/content/11/11932/post_11932394.html |
| Metro extensions notice | September 2025 | Exclude later openings from the June snapshot | https://jtys.sz.gov.cn/zwgk/jtzx/cxtx/content/post_12415875.html |

Sources were consulted on October 18, 2026. The April table contains 899 records and is retained as the analysis input because later website updates could change the comparison. It is a name-based historical check, not a transfer validation sample. Full webpage snapshots are not part of this repository.
