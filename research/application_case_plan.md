# Destination-case definitions

These cases are an exploratory extension of the citywide coverage analysis, not a preregistered sample. All three selected cases are reported.

| Case | Metro station | Bus label | Exact target bus-stop name |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hospital | Qiankeng / 茜坑 | B862路 | 市二医院龙华医院 |
| Industrial park | Xingdong / 兴东 | M518路 | TCL国际E城总站 |
| Office | Qianhaiwan / 前海湾 | B830路 | 前海大厦 |

At each station and journey direction, enumerate all transfer-target pairs satisfying stop order within the 500 m transfer radius, then choose the minimum along-route distance. The baseline ride budget is 5 km and stop buffer is 300 m. Sensitivity uses transfer radii of 300, 500 and 800 m and ride budgets of 3, 5 and 8 km.

Direction-level coverage and exclusive area describe the whole eligible selected bus direction. Target eligibility applies to the exact named stop and does not establish access to an entire facility. Qianhai Tower is a documented bus stop; it is not replaced with the entrance of the building named in the June newspaper story. The hospital's inbound named stop lies just beyond the baseline ride budget; another nearby stop remains eligible.

The official sources provide service context. All geometric computations still use CPTOND-2025 and are not independent geographic validation. No passenger counts or welfare effects are inferred.
