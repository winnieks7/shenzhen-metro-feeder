# Data attribution and licensing

The MIT license covers the original analysis code and documentation. It does not replace third-party data rights.

The original CPTOND-2025 v2 dataset is by L. Wang and colleagues, available at https://doi.org/10.6084/m9.figshare.29377427.v2 under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0): https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

Its accompanying article is Wang et al. (2026), "China Public Transport Operation Network Dataset (CPTOND-2025): National-Scale Bus-Metro Vector Dataset," Scientific Data 13, 188. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41597-025-06505-4.

Original shapefiles are downloaded from Figshare, rather than redistributed here. The result tables, derived geometries and figures in this repository were created by Yefang Zhou, Junyao Zhang and Di Wang from the Shenzhen subset. They involve filtering, coordinate transformation, ordered-route reachability, buffer unions and summarization; these derived outputs are made available under CC BY 4.0 with attribution to both the original data creators and this analysis. The data describe a June 2025 snapshot.

The factual April 2025 bus-route table in `research/official_sources/` was extracted from the Shenzhen municipal government's published route registry. Attribution and its source URL are recorded in `research/official_sources/README.md`. No MIT or CC license is asserted over that third-party source. Full official webpage snapshots and the reprinted newspaper article are not redistributed.
